# Code of Conduct & Responsible Enforcement Pledge

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, professional, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Responsible Legal Enforcement Ethics

`brand-takedown` is built strictly for **legitimate intellectual property owners and their authorized legal representatives** to defend against counterfeit goods, brand impersonation, phishing, and consumer safety hazards.

By contributing to or using this project, community members agree to uphold the following ethical boundaries:

1. **Good-Faith Accuracy**: Never fabricate trademark/patent registration numbers, falsify original publication dates, or submit deceptive sworn perjury statements (`17 U.S.C. § 512(f)` / `EU DSA Art. 16`).
2. **Respect Fair Use & Authorized Resellers**: Never misuse automated takedown workflows to silence legitimate consumer reviews, comparative commentary, nominative fair use, or authorized distribution partners.
3. **Zero Abuse-Queue Spamming**: Respect service provider SLA windows and regulatory filing channels; escalate methodically with documented evidence rather than repetitive automated mail floods.
4. **Strict Confidentiality**: Never expose personal data of third parties or private brand case records in public repository spaces.

## Standards of Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy, accuracy, and rigor when discussing statutory requirements across jurisdictions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code and legal-workflow feedback
- Focusing on reliable, reproducible, standards-compliant engineering

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, personal attacks, or derogatory language
- Publishing private case files, PII, or credentials without explicit authorization
- Proposing features intended to bypass CAPTCHAs, authentication controls, or platform security boundaries

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers reserve the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
