# Responsible use

Use this project only for legitimate rights holders and authorized representatives acting in good faith.
Respect lawful resale, commentary, fair use, nominative use and due process. Do not fabricate rights,
evidence, signatures, dates or provider receipts. Do not use safety reporting to pressure lawful competitors.

Maintain a professional, harassment-free community. Report vulnerabilities privately; do not expose
personal information or confidential brand records in public issues. Do not propose bypassing CAPTCHA,
authentication, access controls or human approval. Review allegations rather than promising outcomes.
